# Code of Conduct

We are committed to making eVoca a welcoming and respectful open-source project.

Participants are expected to:

- be respectful and constructive
- assume good intent while addressing technical disagreements
- focus criticism on ideas, code, and behavior rather than people
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks
- protect private information and secrets shared during project work

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this standard.

For conduct concerns, contact the project maintainers privately through the repository's configured contact channel.
